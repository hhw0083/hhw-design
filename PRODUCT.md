# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, product teams, collaborators, and clients evaluating HHW as a UI/UX designer. They arrive quickly, scan visually first, and need to understand the designer's point of view, craft level, and ability to shape complex product experiences.

## Product Purpose

HHW is a personal image and portfolio site. Its first job is to make the designer feel memorable before the visitor reaches individual case studies: precise, cinematic, and capable of turning abstract product problems into tactile interface systems.

## Brand Personality

Calm, sculptural, and experimental. The voice should feel confident and selective rather than explanatory, with enough motion and spatial depth to suggest design craft without becoming decorative noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS portfolio templates, soft pastel personal-brand pages, busy Dribbble-style grids, and over-literal tech effects. The site should not feel cute, overly colorful, or like a stock landing page.

## Design Principles

- Make the first viewport feel authored, not assembled.
- Let motion reveal intent: scroll and pointer interaction should expose depth, material, and hierarchy.
- Use fewer elements with stronger contrast, scale, and placement.
- Keep the portfolio message clear even when the visual system is atmospheric.
- Respect quietness; the experience should feel controlled, not noisy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for readable text. Honor reduced-motion preferences with a stable composed frame, and keep essential identity and role information available without relying on WebGL or animation.
